1. The Service

Bokly lets you list physical books from your own library and lend them to people nearby. A borrow costs the borrower 59 SEK. When the borrow is completed, the lender earns 30 SEK and Bokly keeps 29 SEK as its service fee.

The loan of a book is between the lender and the borrower. Bokly provides the app, handles the payment and pays out the lender's earnings.

3. Borrowing and Payment

StepWhat happens
Request59 SEK is reserved on your card or wallet (such as Apple Pay) through Stripe. Nothing is charged yet. The lender sees your email address and phone number so they can contact you.
ApproveThe lender approves and the 59 SEK is charged. You get the lender's email address, phone number and drop-off location to arrange the pickup.
Decline, cancel or no answerIf the lender declines, you cancel, or the lender doesn't answer within 5 days, the reservation is released and you aren't charged.
PickupYou confirm in the app that you've picked up the book. Your 26-day borrow period starts.
ReturnYou return the book and mark it as returned. The lender confirms they got it back, and the borrow is completed.

Payments are processed by Stripe. By paying you also agree to Stripe's terms.

Once a request is approved the fee is charged. If the lender doesn't hand over the book, contact support@bokly.app and we'll look into a refund.

  • ✓The first time you cash out, you set up a payout account with our payment provider Stripe (Stripe Connect). 5. Borrow Period and Overdue Books

The borrow period is 26 days, followed by a 3-day grace period. If the book still isn't returned after that, your account is automatically restricted from borrowing.

A lender can also mark a book as not returned, which restricts the borrower's account. To resolve a restriction, return the book or contact support@bokly.app.

6. Condition of Books

Lenders must describe their books honestly. Borrowers must return books in the condition they received them. Disputes about a book's condition are between the lender and the borrower, but you can contact support@bokly.app and we'll try to help.

9. Ending Your Account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. It isn't possible while a book is being borrowed (by you or from you), while a book you borrowed is marked as not returned, or while you have earnings that haven't been paid out.
