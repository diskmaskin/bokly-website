1. The Service

Bokly provides a peer-to-peer book borrowing service through the Bokly iOS app. By using Bokly you agree to these terms.

Bokly lets you list physical books from your personal library and lend them to nearby users for a fee of 75 SEK per borrow. Lenders receive 30 SEK per completed borrow. The remaining 45 SEK is retained by Bokly as a platform fee.

2. Accounts

  • ✓You must be 18 or older to create an account and use the payment features.
  • ✓You must provide a valid phone number before borrowing or approving borrow requests.
  • ✓You are responsible for all activity under your account.

3. Borrow Flow and Payments

Step What happens
RequestBorrower pays 75 SEK via Stripe. The borrow request is created.
ApproveLender approves; borrower receives lender's phone number.
DeclineLender declines; borrower is refunded 75 SEK in full.
Confirm PickupBorrower confirms physical handover; 26-day borrow period begins; lender earns 30 SEK.
ReturnBorrower marks returned; lender confirms receipt; book becomes available again.

Payments are processed by Stripe. By making a payment you also agree to Stripe's Terms of Service.

4. Borrow Period and Overdue Books

The borrow period is 26 days. A 3-day grace period follows before a book is marked overdue. If a book is not returned within the grace period, the account may be restricted from making further borrow requests until the book is returned or the matter is resolved.

5. Condition of Books

Lenders are responsible for listing books in accurately described condition. Borrowers must return books in the same condition they were received. Bokly does not mediate disputes over book condition — these are between the lender and borrower directly.
