4. Who Can See Your Data

Any signed-in Bokly user can see:

  • ✓your library name and the books you’ve made public
  • ✓your location rounded to about 1 km, used to show how far away your books are

When you request to borrow a book, its owner sees your email address and phone number, so they can contact you about the request.

When the owner approves, you see the owner’s email address, phone number and exact location (the drop-off point). This lasts while the borrow is in progress. Contact details that were already sent to you or the other person, for example in an in-app message or email, stay there.

Other users never see your name from Sign in with Apple or Google, and never see your exact location unless you have an approved borrow with them.

6. How Long We Keep It, and Deleting Your Account

We keep your data for as long as you have an account.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes:

  • ✓your profile (name, email, phone number, library name, location) and profile photo
  • ✓your books and reading status
  • ✓your borrow requests and history
  • ✓your earnings history and the link to your Stripe payout account
  • ✓your in-app messages and device tokens
  • ✓your login

Pending borrow requests are cancelled and their payment reservations released, so nobody is charged.

You can’t delete your account while a book is being borrowed, either by you or from you, or while a book you borrowed is marked as not returned. Finish the borrow first, or contact support@bokly.app. If you have earnings that haven’t been paid out, the app asks you to cash them out or give them up first.

What is kept after deletion:

  • ✓Payment records at Stripe (amount, date, payment method details and the related Bokly IDs). We are required to keep bookkeeping records for 7 years under the Swedish Bookkeeping Act.
  • ✓Earnings you gave up when deleting your account: the amount and the related borrow, without your name or account, as our bookkeeping record.
  • ✓Your Stripe payout account, if you set one up. Stripe keeps it, and its records of your payouts, under its own privacy policy and legal obligations. To close it, contact support@bokly.app.
  • ✓Messages and emails already delivered to other users, which may contain your library name, email or phone number from a past borrow.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • ✓access the data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • ✓correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit in the app)
  • ✓delete your data (in the app, see section 6)
  • ✓restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • ✓data portability: get your data in a machine-readable format
  • ✓withdraw consent for location and push notifications at any time in your phone’s settings

To use these rights, email support@bokly.app. We’ll reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), www.imy.se.

8. Children

You must be 18 or older to use Bokly (see the Terms of Service). We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe we have, contact support@bokly.app and we’ll delete it.

9. Changes

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
