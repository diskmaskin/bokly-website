How It Works

Borrowing and lending made simple

Borrow a Book

1

Find a Book Near You

Browse the Explore tab to see books available within 5 km.

2

Request & Pay

Tap Borrow and pay 75 SEK securely. Your request is sent to the owner.

3

Arrange Pickup

Once approved, you get the owner's phone number to arrange a meetup.

4

Return Within 26 Days

Enjoy the book and return it. Declined requests are fully refunded.

Lend Your Books

1

Add Your Books

Scan the ISBN barcode or search by title. Your books appear in the local Explore feed.

2

Receive Requests

Get notified when someone wants to borrow. Review the request and approve or decline.

3

Hand Over the Book

Meet the borrower and hand over the book. Confirm pickup in the app.

4

Get Paid

Earn 30 SEK per borrow, paid out to your account automatically.
