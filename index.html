Why install Bokly

One app. Two ways to get more value from books.

Borrow what you need from someone nearby. Lend what you already have to another reader.

See books near you

Search by title or ISBN and see which physical copies are available nearby.

Pay for the time you need

Borrow the book for 26 days instead of paying the full purchase price.

Collect it locally

Arrange a nearby handover without waiting for packing or delivery.

Earn from your books

List books you have finished and earn when another reader borrows them.

How it works

From one local shelf to the next reader.

Bokly connects both sides and handles discovery, requests and payment. The borrower and owner arrange the local handover.

01

Search or list

Find a book nearby or scan the ISBN to add one from your shelf.

02

Request or approve

The borrower sees the price. The owner sees their earnings and chooses whether to accept.

03

Meet and complete

Hand over locally. The borrower returns the book within 26 days and the owner gets paid.

Trust and protection

Clear steps. Real people. Support when needed.

Before anything changes hands, both people know the price, the return date and who they are dealing with.

01

Verified accounts

Users sign in and verify their phone number before borrowing or approving requests.

02

Secure payment

Payments and lender payouts are handled through Stripe.

03

Clear return period

The 26-day loan period begins only after the physical handover is confirmed.

04

Refunds and support

Declined requests are refunded. Bokly support is available if something goes wrong.
